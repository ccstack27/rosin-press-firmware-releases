Rosin Press Firmware

Installs or updates the firmware on the press controller board over USB.

Open this page in Chrome or Edge

This browser can't talk to USB devices. Use Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on a Windows or Mac computer.

1Before you start

  1. Switch the press off at the breaker and open the cabinet.
  2. Connect a USB-C cable from this computer to the controller board's USB port (J8).

2Connect to the board

Click Connect, choose the port in the list that opens, then click Connect there too.
